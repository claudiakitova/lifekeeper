# Demo

Mira Novak is a made-up person. Her exports in [`exports/`](exports/) are generated by [`make_demo.py`](make_demo.py) to show what Lifekeeper makes from ordinary data:

- a Telegram export, one WhatsApp chat and an Instagram chat
- a Google Takeout zip with photos (and their .json files), location history and Keep notes, plus the newer phone Timeline export
- Facebook, Instagram and X posts (with two retweets, which are left out as other people's words)
- a calendar, Spotify streaming history, Netflix viewing, Goodreads books and LinkedIn jobs
- Google searches, ChatGPT chat titles, a time tracker and a mood diary
- eight notes of her own

Open [`life.html`](life.html) to see the result, or make it again:

```
python toolkit/lifekeeper/lifekeeper.py demo/exports --out demo/life.html
```

To make the exports again (they come out the same every time, on any computer):

```
python demo/make_demo.py
```

Two details are there on purpose:
- Her Facebook and Instagram files copy a real quirk of Meta's exports: text stored as if UTF-8 were Latin-1. Lifekeeper fixes it, which is why her Russian birthday post reads correctly.
- Her LinkedIn says "Designer" where her own note says "junior designer". The page shows both, her own words first, LinkedIn marked as LinkedIn.
