# Privacy

A life archive holds health records, money, relationships and other people's messages. It needs more care than anything else on your computer.

## Keep it local

- Keep the archive on your own computer or on a drive you control.
- By default, nothing goes to an outside service. Search, OCR, transcription and small AI models all run locally. Lifekeeper doesn't use the network at all.
- Sometimes an outside service would do a job better, for example a large AI model reading handwriting. Decide that on purpose, for that one job, and write the decision down. Prefer services that don't train on your data and that let you delete it.

## What stays out of titles, summaries and the timeline

- passwords, keys and tokens
- passport, ID, card and account numbers
- phone numbers and home addresses
- intimate photos, which get a neutral label ("a private photo") and are not described

The original files still contain these. The pages you read don't.

## Secrets in the toolkit

- Keep API keys and tokens in files that the backup and any repository leave out.
- Don't commit an archive, an export or a settings file with keys to a repository, public or private.
- Keep backup passwords in a password manager and on paper. If you lose them, the encrypted backup can't be read.

## Other people are in your data

Your chats hold your friends' words, and your photos hold their faces. Having them in your archive doesn't give you the right to publish them.

- Don't publish an archive, or a report, demo or screenshot made from one.
- Use a made-up person for demos. The demo in this repository is invented.
- Label other people's words as theirs (forwarded posts, saved texts, shared accounts).

## Building an archive for someone else

If you're helping a parent or a friend, get their written agreement first, and treat their data as carefully as your own:

- Tell them what you'll build, where their data will be kept, and which tools you'll use.
- Use an online AI service on their data only if they agree in writing.
- Keep their data apart from yours and out of your own backups.
- Hand over the finished archive and delete your working copy.

There's a short [consent form](download-your-data/consent.md) you can adapt.
