# How to download your data

Download what you want to keep. You don't have to do all of it at once. Apps move their menus around, so if a step isn't where this page says, search the app's help for "download your information" or "export".

Keep the files on your own computer or an encrypted drive. Don't email them, and don't upload them anywhere you didn't choose.

## Telegram (on a computer)
1. Install **Telegram Desktop** and log in.
2. **Settings → Advanced → Export Telegram data.**
3. Tick **Personal chats** (and groups, if you want them), plus **Photos**, **Voice messages** and **Videos** if they matter to you.
4. Format: **Machine-readable JSON**. Click **Export**. It can take a while.

## WhatsApp (on the phone, one chat at a time)
- **iPhone:** open a chat → tap the name at the top → **Export Chat** → **Attach Media** → save it to Files.
- **Android:** open a chat → **⋮ → More → Export chat** → **Include media**.

## Instagram
Profile → **☰ → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information** → **Download or transfer information** → your account → **All available information** → **Download to device** → Format **JSON**, Media quality **High**, Date range **All time** → **Create files**. You'll get an email when it's ready.

## Facebook
**Settings & privacy → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information**, then the same choices as Instagram: **JSON**, **All time**, **High** quality.

## Google (photos, Drive, contacts, calendar, YouTube)
1. Go to **takeout.google.com**.
2. **Deselect all**, then tick what you want: **Google Photos**, **Drive**, **Contacts**, **Calendar**, and **Mail** or **YouTube** if you want them.
3. **Next step → Export once → .zip → Create export.**
4. Your location history now lives on your phone: in **Google Maps → your picture → Your Timeline**, look for **export** in its settings.

## LinkedIn
**Me → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data** → **Download larger data archive**. It arrives by email within a day. LinkedIn is a sales version of your career, so also write down what really happened in your own words.

## Health
- **iPhone:** **Health** app → your picture → **Export All Health Data**.
- **Samsung:** **Samsung Health → Settings → Download personal data**.
- Other apps (sleep, cycle, mood, food) usually have **Export** in their settings.

## Photos
- **iPhone / iCloud:** connect the phone to a computer and copy the photos, or download them from iCloud.com → Photos.
- **Google Photos:** through Google Takeout (above).
- **Old drives and folders:** copy the folders as they are. Don't rename anything.

## Paper documents
Scan with your phone (iPhone: **Notes → camera → Scan Documents**, or a free scanner app), one PDF per document. You don't need to name them.

## For someone else
If you're building an archive for someone else, get their written agreement first. There's a [consent form](consent.md) you can adapt.
