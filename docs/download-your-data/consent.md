# Your life archive: what I'll do with your data

*A template for building an archive for someone else (a parent, a friend). Change it to fit, and give it to them before you touch any of their data.*

**What I'll build:** a private archive of your life from the data you choose to give me: a timeline, the people in your life, places, photos, documents and search. It's yours, in open files, with no subscription.

**Where your data lives:** the finished archive goes on your computer, or on a drive you keep. While I build it, a working copy sits on my computer, kept apart from my own files and out of my backups.

**Tools:** everything runs on my computer. Reading photos and handwritten or scanned documents works better with an online AI service. I'll only use one if you tick the box below.

**What I won't do:** share your data, sell it, train any AI on it, or keep it.

**When it ends:** I hand you the archive and delete my working copy within 14 days. You can ask me to stop and delete everything at any time, no reason needed.

- [ ] Photos
- [ ] Documents and scans
- [ ] Health records
- [ ] It's OK to use an online AI service to read my photos and documents
